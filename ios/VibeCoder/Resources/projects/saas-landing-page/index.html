Frequently Asked Questions

How secure is my data? +
Your data is encrypted end-to-end with AES-256 encryption, the same standard used by banks and government agencies. We implement zero-knowledge architecture, meaning we can't access your files even if we wanted to. All data transfers use TLS 1.3 encryption.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Absolutely! You can cancel your subscription at any time with no penalties or fees. Your data will remain accessible for 30 days after cancellation, giving you plenty of time to download everything you need.
What happens if I exceed my storage limit? +
We'll send you a notification when you reach 80% of your storage limit. If you exceed your limit, you can upgrade to a higher plan or delete some files. Your existing files remain safe and accessible.
Do you offer a free trial? +
Yes! All paid plans come with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. You can experience all premium features before making a commitment.
How does team collaboration work? +
You can create shared folders and invite team members via email. Set granular permissions (view, edit, or admin) for each member. All changes sync in real-time, and you can see who made what changes with our activity log.
Is there a file size limit? +
Individual files can be up to 5GB on the free plan and 50GB on paid plans. For larger files, we recommend using our chunked upload feature which breaks files into smaller pieces for reliable transfer.
