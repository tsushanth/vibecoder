VibeBuild is a free AI app builder for Android. Describe the app you want in plain English, watch the AI build it, preview it right in the app, then publish it to your own yourapp.vibebuild.cc link. No coding needed.

Want to create an app from a prompt? Type something like "a budget tracker with a dark theme" and VibeBuild builds it for you. It is a no-code app maker for people who have an idea but do not write code, and a quick way for developers to try one out. If you like vibe coding, this is vibe coding with a preview and a link: you describe, the AI writes the code.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Describe your app idea in a few words or sentences
2. Watch the build progress live
3. Preview your app right inside VibeBuild
4. Tweak it by chatting with the AI: change colors, features and layout
5. Publish it to the web and share your yourapp.vibebuild.cc link

VibeBuild builds web apps. Your published app runs in any browser; it is not a native app.

FEATURES
- Build apps with AI: describe the idea and get a working web app
- Live preview inside the app
- Tweak with AI chat
- Publish to yourapp.vibebuild.cc: you choose the subdomain
- Save and manage your apps here, and on the web at vibebuild.cc
- Explore apps made by the community
- Idea cards to get you started

FREE FOR NOW
VibeBuild is free for now while we grow: no credit card and no subscription. Fair use applies: up to 30 builds a day and 50 tweaks per project (current limits, they may change). You need a free account (email or Google) to build.

PRIVACY
Your prompts are sent to AI providers to generate your app. Details: vibebuild.cc/privacy
